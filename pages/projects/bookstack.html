BookStack

2022 · Data Science · Library Management App

A group project developed for Data Science and Database courses. We scraped data from Goodreads, trained and compared multiple machine learning models, and stored the results in a library management application called Bookstack.

I got hands-on experience in data scraping, preprocessing, training machine learning models, and data analysis, as well as understanding how these workflows integrate with database-driven applications.

In addition, I also learned the process of writing and publishing an academic paper for a conference.

Even though this isn't a game-related project, I feel like I wouldn't have enjoyed the process of making BookStack as much as I did if it wasn't related to my passion of reading/writing.

Role

Machine Learning Engineer · Data Analyst · Data Scientist

MySQL · Python · Jupyter Notebook
