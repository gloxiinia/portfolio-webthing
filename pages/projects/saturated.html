Saturated

2025 · Unity · 3D · Psychological Horror

drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · shift + drag to pan

A 3D psychological horror game developed by JoyBait Studio for an Apple Developer Academy group project. Inspired by saturation diving and other underwater horror games.

I got to apply the skills I’ve developed over the past eight months while expanding into Blender-based 3D modeling, texture creation, and narrative design.

Working alongside teammates with game industry experience also gave me insight into the full game studio workflow; from early concept brainstorming and defining a target audience to asset creation and showcasing the final game.

Most importantly, this project was my first full-fledged game. Not just a demo, but a fully-published game on the App Store. People were experiencing the world our group has managed to bring to life and I was part of why that happened.
