Tales of Tula

2025 · Godot · 2D · Action-Platformer

A 2D action-platformer game made for an Apple Developer Academy group project. Inspired by folklore and culture in Bali.

I got to work across both narrative and visual development.

I contributed to story writing, tileset design, visual effects such as projectiles and splash effects, and assisted with extra animations where needed.

As my first project using a dedicated game engine, it introduced me to a new development workflow and allowed me to explore more creative roles beyond writing, including animation and asset creation, which, before this, I thought was unrealistic for me to pursue.

Role

Lead Story Writer · 2D Artist
